# Digital Clinic Appointment and Queue Management System

A web-based system designed to simplify and digitize clinic appointment booking and patient queue management.

## 📌 About the Project

Small clinics often manage appointments and patient queues using phone calls, paper registers, and manual communication. This can lead to scheduling conflicts, long waiting times, overcrowding, and difficulty managing patient records.

The Digital Clinic Appointment and Queue Management System provides a centralized platform where patients can book appointments and track their queue status, while clinic staff can manage doctors, appointment slots, daily appointments, and patient queues.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Digitize the clinic appointment booking process.
- Reduce dependency on paper-based records and phone calls.
- Allow patients to view available doctors and appointment slots.
- Provide appointment and queue numbers to patients.
- Allow patients to check their appointment and queue status.
- Reduce patient waiting time and overcrowding.
- Help clinic staff manage appointments and queues efficiently.
- Reduce scheduling conflicts and manual errors.
- Maintain appointment and patient records digitally.

## ✨ Features

### Patient

- Patient registration and login
- View available doctors
- View available appointment slots
- Book an appointment
- Receive appointment/queue number
- Check appointment status
- Cancel or reschedule appointments
- View appointment history

### Clinic Staff / Admin

- Staff/Admin login
- Manage doctors
- Manage appointment slots
- View daily appointments
- Manage patient queue
- Update patient status
- Mark patients as completed, cancelled, or no-show
- View appointment history
- Generate basic reports
